Use Renamix
with confidence.

A simple guide to selecting files, creating rules, previewing changes, and renaming.

01

Select your files

Choose the files you want to rename in Renamix. The selected files appear in the workspace with their current names and preview names.

02

Create your rules

Use the available rules to shape the new filenames. Add sequential numbering, a date, the parent folder, or prefix and suffix text. Rules can be combined.

03

Preview the results

Review the exact new filename for every file before you make a change. The live preview keeps the result clear and easy to check.

04

Rename

When the preview looks right, apply the rename to your selection. If you change your mind, use undo or redo to move between the rename states.
